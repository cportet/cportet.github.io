Profile

A software craftsman with over 28 years of experience, I have held many roles: technical lead, project manager, developer. Equally comfortable in Agile environments and Waterfall approaches, I let user satisfaction and software quality be my only compass.

My expertise lies in Agile project management (Kanban, Scrum), team management and web application development with ASP.NET (MVC or Blazor WebAssembly). I have worked in industry, services, public administration and software publishing, in Picardy, the Paris region, Belgium and now the Lyon area.
